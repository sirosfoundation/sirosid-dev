Open the console How it works

A whole stack

Wallet, wallet backend, credential issuer, verifier, credential registry, trust service and a test login provider, wired together and reachable on their own HTTPS addresses.

Starting points

Ready-made configurations for the standard stack, credential types from the SIROS registry, wallet attestation, the Digital Credentials API and interoperability testing.

Disposable by design

Instances are removed after three days unless you keep them. Stop, start or reset one whenever you like.

Passkeys, no passwords

You sign in with a passkey, and that same passkey protects your saved configurations and instance credentials.

Works with your assistant

Connect an AI assistant such as Claude Code over MCP and let it create and manage your instances for you.

By invitation

Open to SIROS partners and developers. Ask your SIROS contact for an invite.

Who it is for

Teams that build or integrate wallets, issuers and verifiers and need a realistic counterpart to test with: interoperability events, partner integrations, and SIROS's own development. It is a test bench, so everything it issues and verifies is synthetic.

Not for production. Do not put real personal data in an instance. Instances can be erased at any time, and the service makes no availability promise.
SIROS ID Dev is a test and development service run by the SIROS Foundation. It is not a production service and carries no availability or data-retention promise. Access is by invitation.
